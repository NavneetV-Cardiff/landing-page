About Me

I am a bioinformatician with a passion for leveraging computational methods to solve complex biological problems. My work focuses on analyzing large-scale genomic datasets, developing machine learning models for biological predictions, and creating tools that enable researchers to extract meaningful insights from their data.

With expertise in statistical analysis, algorithm development, and data visualization, I strive to make biological data more accessible and actionable for the scientific community.
